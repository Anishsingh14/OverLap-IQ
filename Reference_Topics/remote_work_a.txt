Remote work has become increasingly common, especially following the global shift triggered by the COVID-19 pandemic. Many companies discovered that employees could remain productive while working from home, leading to a lasting change in workplace culture. Benefits of remote work include greater flexibility, reduced commuting time, and improved work-life balance for employees, while employers can save on office space and access a wider talent pool unrestricted by geography. However, remote work also presents challenges such as feelings of isolation, difficulties in team collaboration, and the need for strong self-discipline. As a result, many organizations have adopted hybrid models that combine in-office and remote work to balance these trade-offs.
